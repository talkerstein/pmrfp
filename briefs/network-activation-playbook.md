## The one big idea

PMRFP only works when **both sides show up**: property managers posting RFPs (demand)
and trades who answer them (supply). An empty board kills it. So we don't "spread the
word" evenly — we light up **dense local clusters** where supply and demand can find
each other on day one.

Your 5 contacts aren't 5 favors. They're **3 chess moves**:

| Move | Who | What it builds |
|------|-----|----------------|
| **Lock the GTA** (your beachhead) | Crystal Ball (Eital & Ilan) + Toronto Jewish business community | Toronto supply density + word-of-mouth demand |
| **Open Montreal as beachhead #2** | Elie (brings trades) + your Montreal PM friend (brings RFPs) | A whole second liquid market — supply + demand from one phone call each |
| **Bank the US for later** | St. Louis PMs | Charter advisors + intros now; first market when we launch USD |

Elie + Montreal PM = the highest-leverage pair on this list. One brings the contractors,
one brings the jobs. That's a self-contained market. Prioritize it.
