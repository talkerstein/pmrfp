Step 01

Free email — hi@pmrfp.com

Two jobs: a real inbox you can read/reply from (Google Workspace alias), and an app sending domain (Resend). Both free.

  • Add pmrfp.com as a Workspace alias domain

    10 min

    Google Admin console → Account → Domains → Manage domains → Add a domain → choose "User alias domain" → enter pmrfp.com. This makes hi@pmrfp.com arrive in your existing hi@talkerstein.ca inbox — free, no extra seat.

    DECIDED PATH

  • Verify ownership (TXT) + add Google MX records

    5 min in DNS

    Google gives you 1 verification TXT record and a set of MX records. Add them at your DNS host. Write these MX values down — you must keep them after the Step-3 cutover.

    DNS — do alongside Step 3

  • Turn on "Send mail as" hi@pmrfp.com in Gmail

    3 min

    Gmail → Settings → Accounts → "Send mail as" → Add another email → hi@pmrfp.com. Now you can reply to PMs/trades from the PMRFP address out of your normal inbox.

  • Verify pmrfp.com in Resend + add its DNS records

    10 min

    resend.com → Domains → Add pmrfp.com → add the SPF/DKIM (TXT + CNAME) records it shows. This is what lets the app send the 9 transactional emails. Follow briefs/resend-setup.md.

    UNBLOCKS APP EMAIL

  • Paste Resend env vars into Vercel

    3 min

    Vercel → pmrfp project → Settings → Environment Variables (Production): RESEND_API_KEY=re_… and RESEND_FROM="PMRFP <hi@pmrfp.com>". Redeploy. Then tell me — I'll fire the handoff email that's queued.

Step 02

Flip Stripe to LIVE

Independent of DNS — you can do this right now. You're recreating the 3 prices in live mode and swapping the keys in Vercel.

  • Activate the PMRFP Stripe account

    15 min

    In the PMRFP Stripe account (the separate one, not TCG): toggle to Live mode → complete activation: business details, bank account for payouts, statement descriptor. Stripe also asks for your Terms / Privacy / Refund URLs — have those pages live first.

    REVENUE GATE

  • Recreate the 3 prices in Live mode

    10 min

    Live-mode product IDs differ from test. Create: Trade Pro $249/yr, Trade Pro $29/mo, Featured $599/yr (all CAD). Copy each new price_… ID.

  • Create the Live webhook endpoint

    5 min

    Stripe → Developers → Webhooks → Add endpoint → https://pmrfp.com/api/stripe/webhook (use pmrfp.vercel.app if the domain isn't cut over yet, then update after Step 3). Select exactly these 5 events: checkout.session.completed, customer.subscription.created, customer.subscription.updated, customer.subscription.deleted, invoice.payment_failed. Copy the whsec_… signing secret.

  • Swap the Live keys + price IDs in Vercel

    5 min

    Vercel Production env — these EXACT names (verified against the code): STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_live_…, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_…, STRIPE_PRICE_TRADE_PRO_ANNUAL=price_…, STRIPE_PRICE_TRADE_PRO_MONTHLY=price_…, STRIPE_PRICE_FEATURED_ANNUAL=price_…. No publishable key needed (checkout is server-side redirect). Redeploy.

  • $1 live smoke test → refund

    5 min

    Real card, sign up as a trade, pay, confirm: directory listing goes live + webhook promotes the org to approved + GHL contact appears. Then refund the charge in Stripe.

Step 03

Make pmrfp.com live

The last, public, semi-irreversible move. Repoint DNS to Vercel — don't delete anything, archive the old WordPress first.

  • Export + archive the old WordPress site

    10 min

    Download a full export/backup of the current pmrfp.com WordPress. Keep it 30 days. This is your undo button — you're repointing DNS, not deleting the old host.

    SAFETY NET

  • Add pmrfp.com + www in the Vercel project

    5 min

    Vercel → pmrfp project → Settings → Domains → Add pmrfp.com and www.pmrfp.com. Vercel shows you the exact A record (apex) and CNAME (www) to set — use those, not values from memory.

  • Set the A/CNAME at your DNS host — keep MX intact

    10 min + propagation

    Point apex @ (A) and www (CNAME) at Vercel. Leave the Google MX + Resend TXT/CNAME records from Step 1 untouched. Propagation: minutes to a couple hours.

    THE BIG SWITCH

  • Set NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL + Supabase Auth URLs to pmrfp.com

    5 min

    Vercel env: NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://pmrfp.com (redeploy). Supabase → Auth → URL Configuration → Site URL https://pmrfp.com + redirect https://pmrfp.com/**. Otherwise signup confirmation links point at the old URL.

  • Point Stripe webhook + Resend FROM at the real domain

    5 min

    Update the Stripe Live webhook URL to https://pmrfp.com/api/stripe/webhook (if you used vercel.app in Step 2). Confirm Resend FROM is hi@pmrfp.com.

  • 301 redirects + resubmit sitemap

    15 min

    Map any old WP URLs → new pages (the 7 RFPs, /about, /contact). Verify pmrfp.com in Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml. Protects rankings + kills 404s.

The sequence, one breath

Step 1 (Email) and Step 3 (Domain) share one DNS sitting — add Google MX + Resend records and the Vercel A/CNAME at the same time. Step 2 (Stripe) you can do right now, in parallel, no DNS needed.

The only way to break it: deleting MX records during the domain switch. Don't. A/CNAME = website, MX = email. They coexist.
