## Send cadence

- Connection request: Mon AM
- Follow-up DM if no acceptance after 4 days: pass (don't re-poke)
- DM after acceptance: same day or next AM
- If "tell me more": reply within 4 hours during business hours
- If no reply after 5 business days: send ONE bump ("Still up for that founding spot?") then stop

## Targeting filters on LinkedIn Sales Navigator / Search

- Industry: Construction / Electrical / Mechanical / Industrial
- Job title: Owner OR President OR GM OR VP Operations OR Business Development
- Company size: 11-200 employees (sweet spot — bigger commercial work, not too corporate)
- Location: Greater Toronto Area, Hamilton, Mississauga, Vaughan, Markham
- Keywords: "commercial electrical" OR "ICI electrical" (Industrial-Commercial-Institutional)
