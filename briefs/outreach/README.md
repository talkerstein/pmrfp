# Founder-Led Recruit: 30 GTA Commercial Electricians

**Goal:** Seed PMRFP with 30 comped Trade Pro accounts from real GTA commercial electrical contractors. They get 6 months free Pro. We get: directory listings (proves the platform is real), logos for social proof, 1 referral each, baseline category density in Electrical so the first paying PMs see vendors when they search.

**Why electricians first** (per Construction Expert): most professionalized trade, ESA-licensed (easy to verify), commercial-focused firms exist in large numbers in the GTA, highest ticket per RFP (good $/per-interest economics), least likely to flinch at $249/yr down the road.

**Target close rate:** 30% from a cold list = aim for 100 outreach touches to land 30 signups.

## The offer (one sentence)

> Free 12-month Trade Pro account on PMRFP — Canada's commercial property RFP + vendor platform — in exchange for completing your profile and (when you have time) referring one peer.

## Files in this folder

- `email-cold.md` — Cold email template (3 versions: short / medium / referral-warm)
- `linkedin-dm.md` — LinkedIn DM template + connection-request copy
- `phone-script.md` — 90-second voicemail / 60-second cold call
- `target-list.md` — Starter list of 30+ GTA commercial electrical contractors to research
- `tracking-template.csv` — Spreadsheet for tracking outreach status

## Workflow

1. **Sun night:** open `target-list.md`. Cross-reference with LinkedIn — find owner / GM / business development contact. Verify the company is genuinely commercial-focused (not residential-only).
2. **Mon morning:** send 10 cold emails using `email-cold.md` short version.
3. **Mon afternoon:** send 10 LinkedIn connection requests with note from `linkedin-dm.md`.
4. **Tue:** repeat with next 10. Follow up Mon's emails with email v2 ("just bumping this").
5. **Wed:** 10 more LinkedIn + first phone calls to anyone who hasn't replied.
6. **Thu / Fri:** continue cadence. By end of week 1: ~30 contacts hit.
7. **Week 2:** repeat for non-responders + intro-replies.
8. **Track everything in `tracking-template.csv`.**

## Realistic outcome

- 30 cold touches → 8-12 responses → 4-6 sign up immediately + 2-3 "send me info" → 6-9 closed.
- 60 cold touches → 12-18 closed.
- 100 cold touches → 25-35 closed. **That's the target.**

## Mindset

You're not selling — you're recruiting founding members. **Their listing makes the platform credible for the PAYING trades who come after.** Frame the ask that way. Most will say yes if you make it stupid-easy.
