## Personalization rules

- Always use **first name only** (commercial trades are informal)
- Reference **one specific thing** about their company in V2 if no reply ("saw you handle TPL retrofits — exactly the work that comes through our board")
- Never send between Fri 4pm and Mon 8am (commercial trades don't check email weekends)
- Best send times: Mon-Thu 7-9am or 4-6pm (before/after job sites)
- Subject line stays under 60 chars
- Keep total body under 120 words on V1
