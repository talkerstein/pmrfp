# Phone Script — 60-Second Cold Call

Use after 2 email touches with no reply, OR for high-value targets you'd really like to land.

## Voicemail (if no answer)

> Hi {First name}, this is Rishon from PMRFP. I sent you an email last week about a free 12-month listing on a new commercial property RFP platform we're launching — bringing on 30 GTA electrical contractors as founding members. No pressure, no pitch, just wanted to make sure my email didn't get lost. I'll send a quick text or LinkedIn message to follow up. Thanks {First name} — bye.

**(28 seconds. Practice once.)**

## Live answer — opening 15 seconds

> Hey {First name}, Rishon here from PMRFP — I sent you a note about a free listing on a new commercial property RFP platform for Canadian trades. Catch you at a bad time?

**Branch A — "What's this about?"**
→ Go to "The 45-second pitch" below.

**Branch B — "I'm in the middle of something"**
→ "Totally fine — when's a good 5 minutes? I can call back at {their suggestion} or just send you everything in writing."

**Branch C — "Not interested"**
→ "No problem — appreciate you picking up. Quick one before I go — is electrical work for commercial property management something you'd want more of, or are you full on the residential side?" (gathers intel, often opens door)

## The 45-second pitch

> Quick version: PMRFP is a commercial property RFP and vendor directory we're launching across Canada. Property managers — condos, commercial, multi-unit residential — post their RFPs free. Qualified trades like {Company} see them and express interest. Basically MERX, but built for property management and Canada-first.
>
> I'm bringing on 30 GTA electrical contractors as founding members — free 12-month listing, $249 value. After we hit 100 paid trades, founding pricing locks in at $249 a year forever vs. the planned $399.
>
> The ask is 10 minutes to fill in your profile so the platform looks real when the first PMs arrive. That's it. Worth me sending the signup link?

## If "yes — send me the link"

> Done — sending via email and LinkedIn in the next hour. You'll see "founding member signup" in the subject. Anything I should know about {Company} that would help me make sure your listing shines?

## If "let me think about it"

> Of course — totally understand. Want me to put you on a 5-day follow-up so I'm not in your face? Or just leave the door open and you ping me when you're ready?

## Tips

- **Stand up during the call.** Voice carries authority.
- **Smile.** They can hear it.
- **Mirror their pace.** Contractors talk fast in the morning, slow at end-of-day.
- **Mention one specific thing about their company** ("saw you did the {project type} at {location}") — turns "cold" into "warm" in 5 seconds.
- **Never argue.** If they say no, thank them and ask if they know anyone else who'd want a founding spot.
- **Always offer a way out** ("if this isn't for you, just say so and I'll never bother you again") — this earns trust and increases close rate.

## Best times to call

- **Tue / Wed / Thu** between 6:45-7:30am (in the truck, on the way to site)
- **Tue / Wed / Thu** between 4:30-5:30pm (off site, before going home)
- **Avoid:** Mondays (rough day for contractors), Fridays after 1pm (mentally checked out)
