# Starter Target List — GTA Commercial Electrical Contractors

**IMPORTANT — this is a starter list, NOT verified leads.** You MUST verify each one is:
1. Still in business (check website + LinkedIn last activity)
2. Commercial-focused (not residential-only)
3. GTA-based (HQ or significant service area)
4. Owner/GM reachable via LinkedIn

Then find the right contact person (owner, president, GM, or BD lead) and add their name + LinkedIn URL to the tracking sheet.

## Tier 2 — Smaller commercial-focused (10-50 employees)

Easier close rate — owner makes all decisions.

| Search query to find these on LinkedIn |
|---|
| "commercial electrician" + "Toronto" + 11-50 employees |
| "ICI electrical" + "Greater Toronto Area" |
| "electrical contractor" + "Mississauga / Vaughan / Markham" + commercial keyword |
| Local ECAO (Electrical Contractors Association of Ontario) member directory: https://ecao.org/members |
| Toronto Construction Association member list |

**Recommended sources to build out to 30+:**

1. **ECAO Member Directory** — https://ecao.org/members (filter by region GTA, by trade Electrical)
2. **Toronto Construction Association** — https://www.tca.ca/membership-directory
3. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator search** — see filters in `linkedin-dm.md`
4. **ESA (Electrical Safety Authority) Licensed Contractor search** — https://esasafe.com/contractor-and-licensing-services/find-an-electrical-contractor (filter by GTA postal codes, then cross-reference to commercial work)
5. **Google search:** `"commercial electrical contractor" Toronto site:linkedin.com/company`

## Tier 3 — Specialty / niche (high-value, harder to close)

Worth reaching out individually if any look like a particularly good fit:

- **EV charging electrical specialists** — emerging demand, lots of commercial property work coming
- **Data center electrical contractors** — specialty, premium pricing
- **Healthcare facility electrical** — institutional, big-ticket, conservative buyers
- **Solar / renewable commercial electrical** — growth segment, PM-friendly

## Anti-targets (skip these)

- Pure residential / home services (HomeStars-style) — wrong audience
- National enterprise mega-firms (EllisDon, PCL, Aecon) — too big, won't engage with founding-member play
- Companies with no website or last LinkedIn activity > 12 months old — likely dormant

## How to qualify in 60 seconds

Visit their website. Look for:
- ✅ "Commercial" or "ICI" in the headline / services
- ✅ Project portfolio includes commercial/multi-unit buildings (not just homes)
- ✅ ESA-licensed (Electrical Safety Authority of Ontario)
- ✅ At least one project from the last 12 months on their website
- ✅ Has either a "contact" page with a real name OR LinkedIn presence with active posting

If any 3 ✅ — add to outreach. If only 1-2 — skip.
